## Genes related to Parkinson's & Alzheimer's

I queried RTX‑KG2 (`biolink:gene_associated_with_condition`) for **Parkinson disease** (`MONDO:0005180`) and **Alzheimer disease** (`MONDO:0004975`).

These are large association sets — the KG aggregates GWAS, curated, and text‑mined evidence, so each disease has **thousands** of gene associations, with **1,436 genes shared** between the two. That's far too many to list meaningfully, and most are weak/statistical associations rather than causal drivers. So the raw "genes related to X" list isn't the useful part of the answer — the tremor filter is what makes it tractable.

## Which of these induce tremor

Here's the key finding. Tremor is a *phenotype* (`HP:0001337`), so genes don't reach it through the gene–disease predicate — they reach it via `biolink:causes` / `biolink:predisposes`. Of the 1,436 genes tied to **both** PD and AD, exactly **4** are recorded as inducing tremor:

| Gene | Symbol | Note |
|------|--------|------|
| `HGNC:620` | **APP** | Amyloid precursor protein — the classic AD gene; duplications cause early‑onset AD with parkinsonism/tremor |
| `HGNC:11427` | **STUB1** | CHIP ubiquitin ligase — ataxia/neurodegeneration syndromes featuring tremor |
| `HGNC:5286` | **HTR1A** | Serotonin 1A receptor — serotonergic modulation of tremor |
| `HGNC:12692` | **VIM** | Vimentin |

**APP** is the standout — it's a well‑established link across all three (AD, parkinsonism, and tremor), which is a good sanity check on the result.

## Caveats
- `causes`/`predisposes` edges in KG2 lean heavily on text‑mined (SemMedDB) evidence, so treat VIM/HTR1A as leads to verify rather than settled facts. APP and STUB1 have strong independent literature support.
- The "1,436 shared genes" reflects broad association, not causation.
