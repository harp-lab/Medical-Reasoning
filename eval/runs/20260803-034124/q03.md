## Answer

## No — the knowledge graph does not say Imatinib *treats* asthma.

I queried RTX-KG2 for any direct edge between the full Imatinib concept-set (`CHEBI:45783` + 32 equivalent CURIEs) and the asthma concept-set (`MONDO:0004979` + equivalents), in either direction. There is exactly **one** connecting edge:

| Subject | Predicate | Object |
|---|---|---|
| Imatinib | `biolink:affects` | asthma |

**What this means:** the graph asserts Imatinib *affects* asthma, not that it *treats* it. `biolink:affects` is a generic causal/influence relation — it captures that there's a documented biological or research link (Imatinib is a tyrosine-kinase inhibitor and has been studied for its effect on mast cells / airway inflammation in asthma), but the KG does **not** carry a `biolink:treats` edge, which is what would represent an established therapeutic indication.

So, grounded in this knowledge base: Imatinib is *implicated in / studied against* asthma, but it is **not asserted as a treatment** for it. Its actual `treats` indications are cancers like chronic myeloid leukemia (BCR-ABL) and GIST.
